## CONCULSION

In conclusion, I think I was wrong about my hypothesis that the map you play on affects your KD ratio. At first I thought that some maps would make players get a higher KD because they might be easier to play on than others. But after looking at my data, I realized that there isnt really a clear pattern that proves this is true. For example, Villa had a KD ratio of 3.6 in one match but also had a KD of 1.7 in another match. ThemePark also had different KD ratios depending on the match. This shows that playing on the same map doesnt mean you will always get a similar KD ratio. I also noticed that the ranks were different throughout the data, which could have affected the results too. Players might have different skill levels, strategies, or just have a good or bad game. There are probably alot of other things that affect KD ratio besides the map. Overall, my data shows that my original hypothesis was incorrect because the map alone doesnt seem to have a big impact on KD ratio. Even though some maps had higher KD ratios than others, the results werent consistent enough to prove that the map was the reason. If I did this experiment again, I would collect more data and maybe look at other factors like rank or player skill to see what actually affects KD ratio. This helped me understand that just because I think something is true doesnt mean the data will support it.
